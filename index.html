Dosawalla privacy policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

Dosawalla has no accounts. Your game is saved on this phone, and in the phone’s own backup if you use one, so a new phone can carry on. We never see it.

Players 18 and over can choose to watch an ad from Google AdMob for a reward in the game. In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent first, and Privacy choices in Settings changes it. While an ad loads and plays, Google may collect information about the phone, such as its advertising ID and IP address, as Google explains at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Players under 18 never see ads: the game never starts Google’s ad library for them, so it sends nothing about them. The game asks for your birth year once, only to decide this, and keeps it with your game: on this phone, and in the phone’s own backup if you use one.

Questions? Write to the email address on Dosawalla’s Google Play page.

What the game keeps on your phone

Your progress (the stops you’ve played, your stars, money and upgrades), your settings (sound and music on or off), and for the ads the birth year you typed and how many ads you’ve watched today. They stay in the app’s own storage on your phone. Uninstalling Dosawalla deletes them from the phone.

Ads

Dosawalla shows an ad only when a player who is 18 or over chooses to watch one, to double a day’s money or the daily gift. The ads come from Google AdMob. When one loads and plays, Google’s ad library collects information about the phone to show it, count it and stop fraud: the phone’s advertising ID, its IP address (from which Google may tell roughly where it is), how the ad was used, and diagnostics if something goes wrong. That information goes to Google, not to us, and Google’s own policy covers it. In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, Google first asks whether you agree to ads based on your interests; Privacy choices in the game’s Settings lets you change your answer. In US states whose laws let you opt out of ads based on your data, such as California, Privacy choices lets you do that too. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone’s settings.

Your phone’s backup

Android can back up your apps to your Google account. If yours does, Dosawalla’s save, settings and ad answers go in that backup too, and a new phone signed in to the same account picks up where you left off. That backup is Google’s service, kept in your own account; we never receive it. You can turn it off in your phone’s settings.

Children

Children can play all of Dosawalla. Before its first ad, the game asks the player’s birth year, once. Players under 18 never see ads: the game never starts Google’s ad library for them, so nothing about them is sent anywhere. The birth year is kept with the game’s save, on the phone and in its backup.

If this changes

If what Dosawalla collects ever changes, this page will say what, why and who with, before that version reaches your phone.
