Dosawalla privacy policy

Last updated 13 November 2026

Dosawalla collects nothing about you: no name, no email, no location, nothing. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and the game never sends anything over the internet.

Your game is saved on this phone, and in the phone’s own backup if you use one, so a new phone can carry on. We never see it.

Questions? Write to the email address on Dosawalla’s Google Play page.

What the game keeps on your phone

Your progress (the stops you’ve played, your stars, money and upgrades) and your settings (sound and music on or off). They stay in the app’s own storage on your phone. Uninstalling Dosawalla deletes them from the phone.

Your phone’s backup

Android can back up your apps to your Google account. If yours does, Dosawalla’s save goes in that backup too, and a new phone signed in to the same account picks up where you left off. That backup is Google’s service, kept in your own account; we never receive it. You can turn it off in your phone’s settings.

Children

Dosawalla collects nothing from anyone, children included.

If this changes

If Dosawalla ever collects anything (if it shows ads, say), this page will say what, why and who with, before that version reaches your phone.

Who makes Dosawalla

Dosawalla is made by Bisani Ventures. For anything about privacy, write to krishna@bisaniventures.com.
